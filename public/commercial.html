Plans and limits

No paid plan, price, billing currency, renewal period or checkout is configured. No subscription is sold here. Limits: 1.5 MB per workspace, 5,000 CSV rows per import, 5 MB per receipt, 100 MiB and 1,000 receipts per account, 20 per transaction, 90 authenticated API requests per minute. Manual tracking, calculated Mona search and original-currency exports are available. Bank sync, live rates, external AI and money movement are unavailable.

Cancellation and refunds

No paid MONETA subscription exists to cancel. No refund workflow or refund commitment has been approved. Deleting MONETA data does not cancel external services or delete your ChatGPT identity.

Support and contact

Signed-in users with a saved workspace can submit feedback in Settings. It is stored for the owner; email delivery, staffed support and response times are not configured. A public contact and account-access support route are launch blockers.
