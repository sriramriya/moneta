Privacy notice

MONETA uses your supported ChatGPT identity to authorize access. Your name and confirmed regional preferences, financial records and submitted feedback are stored in the site's Cloudflare D1 database, keyed to your authenticated site identity. No passwords are collected by MONETA.

Only the signed-in user's workspace is read or updated through the app's APIs. Mona's calculated search processes those records on the backend. No external AI or exchange-rate provider is configured. Receipt files are stored in protected Cloudflare R2 storage with owner-scoped database metadata. Each file is limited to 5 MB; PNG, JPEG or PDF signatures are checked, without malware scanning. Account limits are 100 MiB, 1,000 receipts and 20 per transaction. Receipt extraction is not configured. Demo data stays separate in session storage. Legacy browser records remain on the original device until you explicitly import or remove them.

Chat conversation is kept only in the current page's memory and can be cleared. Feedback you explicitly submit is stored with your identity; avoid including private financial details. Export or delete your MONETA cloud data in Settings. JSON backup excludes receipt binaries. Failed receipt cleanup remains pending in a durable queue and can be retried after reload. Operator record-and-receipt restoration has been tested in isolated adapters, but no provider backup schedule, encryption/retention or complete recovery service is configured. Deleting MONETA data does not delete your ChatGPT identity. Hosting providers may retain operational backups according to their policies; the app cannot independently purge those backups.

Terms of use

MONETA is a personal record-keeping tool. You are responsible for the accuracy and completeness of entered records and for retaining exports. Debt and simulation figures are estimates based on entered assumptions. The service does not connect to banks, move money, provide personalized investment advice or guarantee outcomes.

Each record retains its currency. Currency totals are reported separately without exchange conversion. No paid plans, payment processing, email reminders or SMS services are configured. These terms describe the current release; no separate company contact or commercial contract has been supplied.

Contact & feedback

For a website issue or an incorrect calculated answer, open Settings and submit feedback. Sign-in is required to save feedback. Feedback is stored for the site owner; no email delivery or response time is promised. No public support email has been configured.

Meet Mona

Mona helps you navigate to transactions, budgets, goals, bills and settings. After sign-in, it can search your authorized records and calculate financial summaries. Generative AI is unavailable until a provider is configured; calculated answers are labeled and link to their source pages.
