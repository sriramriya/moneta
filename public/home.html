A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

How is my financial information stored?

Personal workspaces are stored in the site's cloud database under your authenticated account. Demo records are separate and stay in your browser tab. Mona's calculated search reads your authorized records; no AI provider receives them in this release.

Which currencies can I use?

Select supported ISO currencies and your own number locale. Each record keeps its original currency. Without an exchange-rate provider, reports show separate currency totals and never combine them.

Is Mona live AI?

No generative AI provider is configured. Mona clearly labels that state and offers calculated financial answers, search and navigation. It does not change records.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export transactions as CSV or download a full JSON backup. Editable XLSX exports and browser Print / Save PDF are available.

How do accounts and sign-in work?

Use the supported Continue with ChatGPT sign-in. MONETA stores your confirmed preferences with your account. This site retains its current private access policy; adding a landing page does not make it publicly shared.
