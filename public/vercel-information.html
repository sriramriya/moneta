Data and privacy

This Vercel deployment uses Clerk for real account signup/sign-in and session verification, Neon Postgres for owner-scoped financial records/preferences/feedback, and private Vercel Blob storage for receipt files. The previous Cloudflare-hosted deployment and its data remain separate; no existing customer records have been migrated or linked by email.

Mona performs calculated search of your signed-in records; no generative AI provider, bank synchronization, exchange-rate feed, live market feed, payment execution or external notification provider is configured. Chat remains in page memory. Use fictional data during testing.

Receipt files are signature checked as PNG/JPEG/PDF up to 5 MB, not malware scanned. Uploads are split into authenticated small requests for Vercel's body limit. Incomplete uploads temporarily reserve quota and are cleaned up through a private maintenance job. Downloads require owner authorization and use a short-lived private storage download link; do not share that link. JSON backups exclude receipt binaries.

Settings can export or delete MONETA records. Deletion removes live records/receipt metadata and queues file deletion; failed storage cleanup remains pending. It does not delete your external Clerk account, browser downloads or provider backups. No retention/recovery SLA has been approved. Provider policies and operational backups require owner review.

Terms and availability

Manual financial tracking only. Estimates depend on your entered dates and amounts. Each record keeps its currency; no conversion or combined-currency total is provided. Nothing moves money. No paid plan, billing/refund workflow, service guarantee or commercial source license has been finalized.

Support

Signed-in users with a saved workspace may submit feedback in Settings. It is stored for the owner; no staffed support address or response time is configured. Report test problems privately using fictional/redacted examples. Operator legal identity, public contact and final privacy/terms remain owner decisions.
